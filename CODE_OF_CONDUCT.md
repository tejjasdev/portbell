# Code of Conduct

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1,
which is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).*

## Our pledge

We want participation in portbell to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect other viewpoints and experiences.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for
behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Report problems privately by email to **hey.tejjas@gmail.com**. All reports will be
reviewed and handled with care and discretion, and the privacy of the reporter respected.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when someone represents the project in public.
